# 💼 IBM HR Analytics: Employee Attrition & Performance Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze employee attrition and performance trends at IBM — focusing on understanding workforce demographics, job satisfaction, overtime patterns, and attrition by key HR metrics.

---

## 📝 Short Description / Purpose
The IBM HR Analytics Dashboard provides a comprehensive view of employee attrition drivers within the organization. It helps HR professionals and management visualize the relationship between job satisfaction, age, department, business travel, and attrition rate.  
This tool supports **data-driven decision-making** to enhance employee retention and workforce performance.

---

## 🧰 Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

- 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Main data visualization tool used to design and build the dashboard.  
- 📂 **Power Query** – For data transformation, cleaning, and shaping the HR dataset.  
- 🧠 **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Used for calculated measures (e.g., attrition %, average satisfaction).  
- 🧩 **Data Modeling** – Relationships between employee details, departments, and attrition data were modeled for accurate filtering.  
- 📁 **File Format** – `.pbix` (Power BI project file), `.csv` (dataset), and `.png` (dashboard preview).

---

## 📊 Data Source

**Dataset:** [IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance Dataset (Kaggle)](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/pavansubhasht/ibm-hr-analytics-attrition-dataset)

The dataset contains details of **1,470 employees**, including demographics, job satisfaction, salary, performance, and attrition status.

**Key Columns:**  
- Age, Department, EducationField, JobRole, MaritalStatus  
- BusinessTravel, OverTime, MonthlyIncome, Attrition, etc.  

---

## 🌟 Features / Highlights

### **Business Problem**
IBM wanted to understand the **factors contributing to employee attrition** and identify patterns in workforce demographics that influence turnover.

---

### **Goal of the Dashboard**
To provide a visual, interactive analytical tool that enables HR teams to:
- Analyze attrition by department, role, and demographics  
- Identify high-risk employee segments  
- Support retention strategies through data insights  
- Track employee satisfaction and performance metrics  

---

### **Walkthrough of Key Visuals**

#### 🔹 Top KPIs (Header)
- **Total Employees:** 1.47K  
- **Average Job Satisfaction:** 2.73  
- **Average Age:** 36.92 years  
- **Average Hourly Rate:** 65.89  

#### 🔹 Attrition by Job Role (Bar Chart)
Shows attrition count per job title.  
- *Top Roles with Attrition:* Sales Executive (326), Research Scientist (292), Lab Technician (259)

#### 🔹 Attrition by Education Field
Highlights which education backgrounds experience higher attrition.  
- *Top Fields:* Life Sciences (606), Medical (464)

#### 🔹 Attrition by Department
Displays employee attrition across departments.  
- *Highest Attrition:* Research & Development (961 employees)

#### 🔹 Attrition by Business Travel
Shows attrition rates across travel frequency.  
- Majority attrition from employees who travel rarely (70.9%)

#### 🔹 Attrition by OverTime
Highlights that **28.3%** of employees working overtime show higher attrition.

#### 🔹 Attrition by Marital Status
Indicates that single employees have the highest attrition (45.78%), followed by married (31.9%) and divorced (22.2%).

---

## 💡 Business Impact & Insights

- **Retention Strategy:** Identifies roles and education fields with high attrition for targeted retention efforts.  
- **Workload Management:** Overtime strongly correlates with higher attrition—indicating the need for better work-life balance policies.  
- **Recruitment Focus:** Reveals job roles (e.g., Sales Executives, Scientists) where attrition rates are critical, guiding hiring priorities.  
- **Data-Driven HR:** Enables IBM HR to make evidence-based decisions to reduce turnover and improve satisfaction.
